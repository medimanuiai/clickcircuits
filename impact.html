Where it started.

We started this initiative in the summer of 2023, running sessions in libraries, community centers, and even in our garage, wherever there is a student curious about engineering. We work with libraries, parents, and community programs that serve students with limited access to STEM resources, from our own community to young engineers in India.

Every student gets real components, Arduino boards, sensors, and circuits, not simulations. They learn to build, code, and debug actual electrical systems, and walk away with lasting skills and the confidence that they can create engineering solutions.
