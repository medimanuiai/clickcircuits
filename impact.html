Our Impact

Engineering access, measured in students reached.

100% Free to participating students
Arduino Core platform we teach on
Hands‑on Every session, real components

Click Circuits partners with schools and community programs to put real circuit boards, sensors, and code in front of students who wouldn't otherwise have that chance. We started this initiative in the summer of 2023, running sessions in libraries, community centers, and even in our garage—wherever there's a student curious about engineering. Every session is measured by one thing: did a student build something themselves. From local students in our community to young engineers in India, we're working to bring real opportunities to anyone who wants to discover what they can create.

Our Growing Impact

Over 175 students have participated in Click Circuits sessions and built their first projects with real Arduino boards and sensors.

We partner directly with libraries, parents, and community programs that serve students with limited access to STEM resources, bringing hands-on learning where it's needed most.

Every student gets real components—Arduino boards, sensors, and circuits—not simulations. They learn to build, code, and debug actual electrical systems.

Students walk away with lasting skills and the confidence that they can create engineering solutions.
