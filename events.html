Talks

Bring a talk to your students.

Click Circuits started by putting real Arduino boards in front of students. The same belief drives the talks: people learn technology best when it is approachable, hands-on, and relevant to their own lives.

Talks are tailored to the audience and can be given in person or, where it makes sense, online.

Talk topics

Three questions every audience asks.

01

Why AI matters

What AI is, where it already shows up in everyday life, and why understanding it is becoming a basic skill for everyone.

02

Using AI well

Practical ways to bring AI into school, work, and personal projects, while staying curious, critical, and responsible.

03

Building with AI

How to move from using AI tools to developing with them, using the same learn-by-building approach as our Arduino sessions.
