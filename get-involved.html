Get involved

Help open the door to more possibilities.

Click Circuits runs on people who care about access to engineering and AI education. There are three ways to help.

  • Volunteer

    Help at a workshop, support an event, or share a skill with students. Tell us a little about yourself and how you would like to help.

    Volunteer with us

  • Partner With Us

    Schools, clubs, and community groups can bring a workshop or a talk to their students. Tell us about your group, location, and dates.

    Start a conversation

  • Support Our Mission

    Donate a kit to put real components in students’ hands. One kit helps five students build their first circuits.

    Donate a kit
