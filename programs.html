Programs

Workshops, kits, and talks.

Three ways we put engineering and AI in students' hands, all built around one idea: people learn technology best when they get to build with it.

See upcoming eventsGet involved

Program 01

Hands-on STEM workshops

Small-group sessions where students wire circuits, write code, and test what they built.

  • Real Arduino boards, sensors, and components
  • Building, coding, and debugging real circuits
  • Every session is measured by one thing: did a student build something themselves
See upcoming workshops

Program 02

Arduino and electronics kits

Students get real components to keep learning with, not just a lesson to watch.

  • A kit includes a board, LEDs, resistors, servos, a breadboard, a joystick, and wires
  • 102 boards deployed so far
  • Parts are bought in bulk, so one donated kit helps five students

Program 03

AI and technology talks

Brijesh speaks to schools, clubs, and community groups about AI in plain language, with real examples.

  • Why AI matters: what it is and where it already shows up in everyday life
  • Using AI well: bringing it into school, work, and projects while staying curious and responsible
  • Building with AI: moving from using AI tools to developing with them
