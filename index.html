Who we are

Making hands-on technology education more accessible.

Click Circuits is an initiative that puts Arduino boards, sensors, and real components in front of students who might not otherwise get to try them.

It was founded by Brijesh Murugesan, who also speaks about why AI matters, how to use it well, and how to build with it. The workshops and the talks share one idea: people learn technology best by doing.

Our belief

Curiosity is everywhere. Opportunity should be, too.

A student does not need expensive equipment or a head start to begin. They need a chance to try, and someone to say yes.

  • Real parts, not simulations

    Students work with real Arduino boards, sensors, and circuits, and learn to build, code, and debug them.

  • Access before background

    We bring the tools to students who have had limited access to them.

  • From circuits to AI

    The same learn-by-building approach carries from a first LED to understanding and developing with AI.
