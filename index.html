Our Mission

Click Circuits has two goals: give students hands-on access to engineering, and help people understand AI — why it matters, how to use it well, and how to build with it.

Brijesh Murugesan leads the initiative. It started with free Arduino kits and hands-on lessons in circuits, sensors, and robotics for students who lacked access to them, and it is growing to include public speaking on the importance of AI, how to incorporate it, and how to develop it.

Our goal is to make learning accessible, encourage curiosity, and spark an interest in engineering and AI that students and audiences carry into the future.
