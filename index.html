Our Mission

We believe every student should have the opportunity to discover the world of electrical engineering.

For many students, the opportunity to explore engineering is limited by the cost of parts, equipment, and educational resources. Amshul Medisetti founded this initiative to help bridge that gap, bringing electrical engineering directly to underserved communities.

Through free Arduino kits and hands-on lessons in circuits, sensors, and robotics, we want to give students the chance to experience engineering for themselves. Our goal is to make learning accessible, encourage curiosity, and hopefully spark an interest in engineering that students can carry with them into the future.
