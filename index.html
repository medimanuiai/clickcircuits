What comes in a kit

Everything a student needs to build a first circuit.

Each kit lets a student wire, code, and control their own circuits. Parts are bought in bulk, so one kit helps five students.

  • Arduino board with Bluetooth module

    One microcontroller board that runs the student’s code.

  • 8 LEDs and 8 resistors

    From a mixed assortment pack, for basic circuits.

  • 3 micro servos

    Motors for arms, wheels, and other moving mechanisms.

  • Breadboard

    A solderless board for wiring components together.

  • Joystick

    A dual-axis module for directional control input.

  • Push button

    A momentary button used as a switch or input trigger.

  • Jumper wires

    Male-to-male, male-to-female, and female-to-female.

Who we are

Founded by Brijesh Murugesan.

Click Circuits is an initiative that puts Arduino boards, sensors, and real components in front of students who might not otherwise get to try them.

Brijesh also speaks about why AI matters, how to use it well, and how to build with it. The workshops and the talks share one idea: people learn technology best by doing.
