Put real components in a student’s hands.

Donated kits make hands-on lessons possible for students who otherwise lack access to parts and tools. Thank you for supporting Click Circuits.

What is in 1 kit 1 kit helps 5 kids

Every kit gives students parts to build, wire, and control their own circuits. Because parts are bought in bulk, one kit purchase stretches to cover 5 students.
