Donate a kit

Put real components in a student's hands.

Donated kits enable hands-on lessons for students who otherwise lack access to parts and tools.

Thank you for supporting Click Circuits. Every kit gives students the parts to build, wire, and control their own circuits.

What's in 1 Kit 1 kit helps 5 kids

Every kit is built to give students hands-on parts to build, wire, and control their own circuits. Because parts are bought in bulk, one kit purchase stretches to cover 5 students.

Joystick

Wires

We buy parts in bulk because it makes each component significantly cheaper, which is how one kit purchase is able to help 5 kids instead of just one.
